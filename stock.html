📖 How to Add New Products to ZENVORA in 2 Simple Steps

  1. Add to products.js: Open products.js and add a new item object into the productsData array with your desired title, price, images, sizes, and colors.
  2. Create Product Detail HTML File (in root): Copy an existing template (e.g. ladies-black-suit.html), rename it to match your product's slug (e.g. my-new-dress.html), and call initProductDetailPage("your-new-product-id") at the bottom!
